## Description (paste)

**Every prospect feels "kind of good" until you score them.**

You research. You write. You still don't know who deserves the call. Spreadsheets hide priority. Salesforce takes months. So Monday goes to the loud account — not the ready one.

**Revenue Pipeline System (HUNTER CRM)** — Consultant edition by Freeman Intelligence. A **copyable Airtable pipeline** plus a **Claude scoring rubric** so each lead earns **0–100 points** and a tier: **HOT · HIGH · MEDIUM · WATCH**. Log every touch so you never double-email the same champion.

**Built for** consultants, boutique agencies (&lt;~20 people), and fractional sellers on **$3K+** projects and retainers. **Not for** B2C, enterprise Salesforce teams, or scraped lead lists. Scores are decision support — **not revenue guarantees**.

### Scoring (reason-why)

Seven dimensions → 100 points: Problem Severity · Buying Signal · Ability to Pay · Service Fit · Accessibility · Urgency · Competitive Pressure.

**Tiers:** HOT ≥90 · HIGH ≥75 · MEDIUM ≥60 · WATCH below.
