## Description (paste)

**Know who's HOT before Monday.**

Which prospect deserves your call? When every lead has the same priority in a spreadsheet, you sort by noise — not fit. This kit installs one mechanism: **evidence → weighted score → tier → sort**.

**Three outcomes**

1. **Rank** — 0–100 from seven dimensions (weights sum to 100; Claude rubric included).  
2. **Sort** — HOT · HIGH · MEDIUM · WATCH (thresholds in Airtable).  
3. **Log** — Outreach Log so you don't re-pitch the same decision-maker.

**Revenue Pipeline System (HUNTER CRM)** — Consultant edition by Freeman Intelligence. A **copyable Airtable pipeline** plus a **Claude scoring rubric** for consultants, boutique agencies (&lt;~20 people), and fractional sellers on **$3K+** projects and retainers.

**Not for** B2C, enterprise Salesforce teams, or scraped lead lists. Scores are decision support — **not revenue guarantees**. Example companies in the kit are **fictional**.
