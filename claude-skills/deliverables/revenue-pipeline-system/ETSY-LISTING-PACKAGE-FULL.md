#### Why this exists

Most consultants don’t need another CRM subscription — they need **priority**. When every prospect feels “kind of good,” you burn hours on outreach that was never going to close, or you ignore a HOT account because it wasn’t loud on LinkedIn.

This system separates **research**, **scoring**, and **outreach** into three linked tables so you can defend your pipeline to yourself (or a partner) with numbers, not vibes. The Claude prompt is deliberately rigid: it scores only from evidence you paste in, flags thin research, and outputs a tier you can sort in Airtable.

#### How it works

1. **Copy the Airtable template** using the link in Setup Guide.
2. **Edit your Service Catalog** with your real offers (seven consultant examples included to copy from).
3. **Add 5–10 target accounts** with decision-maker names, detected problems, and evidence signals.
4. **Run the Claude scoring prompt** — paste your catalog + opportunity blocks; get dimension scores, total, tier, and gaps to research.
5. **Work the HOT and HIGH views first** — use Outreach Log for every email/DM; link the opportunity; pick strategy A–F from the template doc.
6. **Re-score after new intel** — tier can move when hiring news, funding, or pain signals show up in your notes.

**Outreach Log (why it matters)**  
Boutique sellers lose deals quietly by contacting the same champion twice with different angles, or by forgetting who already declined. Each row logs date, channel, strategy, and outcome — linked back to the opportunity record. It’s lightweight CRM discipline without workflow automation you’ll never maintain.

**Service Catalog (why it matters)**  
Scoring needs to know what you sell. The template includes a consultant-style catalog (audits, sprints, retainers, positioning work) you replace with your offers. Better catalog copy → better **Service Fit** scores → fewer false HOT leads.

**Scoring model (100 points, 7 dimensions)**

- Problem Severity (0–25)
- Buying Signal (0–20)
- Ability to Pay (0–15)
- Service Fit (0–15)
- Accessibility (0–10)
- Urgency (0–10)
- Competitive Pressure (0–5)

**Default tiers:** HOT ≥90 · HIGH ≥75 · MEDIUM ≥60 · else WATCH. You can adjust formulas in Airtable if your business needs different cutoffs.
