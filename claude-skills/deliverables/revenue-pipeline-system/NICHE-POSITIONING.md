# Niche positioning — Revenue Pipeline System (HUNTER CRM)

## Short answer

**Yes, go narrower than “all B2B.”**  
Best wedge for a **$97** self-serve template:

### Primary ICP (market and copy)

**High-ticket B2B professional services** — people who sell **projects and retainers**, not shopping carts.

| Segment | Typical deal | Why this product fits |
|---------|--------------|------------------------|
| **Management / ops consultants** | $5K–$40K | Few leads, high stakes, scoring beats “gut feel” |
| **Boutique agencies** (marketing, brand, dev shops **&lt;20 people**) | $3K–$25K/mo or project | New-business owner isn’t a Salesforce admin |
| **Fractional CMO / CRO / RevOps** | $4K–$15K/mo | Pipeline is the product; need tier discipline |
| **Specialist firms** (HR, compliance, IT services) | $10K+ | Long cycles; outreach log prevents duplicate touches |

**Headline niche (use on Gumroad):**  
*“For consultants and boutique agencies who sell $3K+ engagements — not for ecommerce or SDR teams living in HubSpot.”*

### Who to **exclude** in copy (reduces bad reviews)

- B2C / ecommerce  
- Enterprise teams with dedicated RevOps + Salesforce  
- “I need 10,000 leads scraped” buyers  

### Better than generic B2B — without shrinking TAM too much

| Too broad | Sweet spot | Too narrow (save for add-on pack) |
|-----------|------------|-----------------------------------|
| “Any B2B” | **Consultants + agencies + fractional executives** | Automotive Tier 2 suppliers only |

Your **internal** HUNTER base can stay industrial; the **customer template** should ship the **consultant Service Catalog** (`SERVICE-CATALOG-CONSULTANTS.md`) + **3 example opportunities** (`EXAMPLE-OPPORTUNITIES.md`).

### Optional future SKUs (not v1 — increases perceived 10/10 over time)

| Add-on | Price idea | Contents |
|--------|------------|----------|
| Industrial / manufacturing pack | +$27 or bundle $117 | Your 7 industrial services + scoring cues |
| Agency new-business pack | +$27 | Retainer + pitch + RFP angles |
| “Score my list” done-with-you | $197–297 | 60-min + you score 10 leads live |

## Messaging angles that convert

1. **Anti-CRM:** “Airtable pipeline + Claude — no 6-month Salesforce implementation.”  
2. **Priority:** “Know who’s HOT before you write another cold email.”  
3. **Proof of work:** “Log every touch; never double-DM the same COO.”  
4. **AI with guardrails:** “Structured 100-point rubric, not ‘write me a sales plan.’”

---

## One-line positioning

**Revenue Pipeline System (HUNTER CRM)** helps **consultants and small agencies** score and prioritize **high-ticket B2B leads** in Airtable, with a Claude prompt that turns messy research into **HOT / HIGH / MEDIUM / WATCH**.
