# Service Catalog — B2B consultants & boutique agencies (default template)

**Use this as the default rows** in the customer-facing Airtable template (replace industrial SKUs).  
Buyer edits prices and names to match their practice.

---

## Row 1 — Discovery & pipeline audit

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Service Name** | Revenue Pipeline Audit (2 weeks) |
| **Short Description** | Map ICP, offer ladder, and current lead sources; deliver scored target list of 25 accounts and a 90-day outreach plan. |
| **Typical Price Range** | $2,500–$5,500 |
| **Target Verticals** | Professional services, SaaS &lt;200 employees, Agencies |
| **Solves Problems** | Random outreach; no scoring; founder doing all sales; pipeline in head or scattered sheets |
| **Ideal Buying Signals** | New positioning; “we need more leads”; first sales hire; plateaued revenue |
