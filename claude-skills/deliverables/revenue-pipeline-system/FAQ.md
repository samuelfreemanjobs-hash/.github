# Buyer FAQ — Revenue Pipeline System (HUNTER CRM)

## Do I need a paid Airtable plan?

No for basic use. Paid tiers help if you want more automations, sync, or seats.

## Does this replace HubSpot / Salesforce?

No — it’s for teams **before** or **beside** heavy CRM: consultants, small agencies, fractional operators. If you have RevOps and Salesforce, this isn’t for you.

## Who is this for?

**Consultants and boutique agencies** selling **$3K+** projects or retainers who need **scored prioritization** and outreach logging, not enterprise pipeline theater.

## What’s “HUNTER CRM”?

Internal codename for the same system. Customer-facing name: **Revenue Pipeline System (HUNTER CRM)**.

## How long to set up?

About **45 minutes** for Day 1 (copy base, edit Service Catalog, add 5 leads, score once). See Quick Start PDF.

## Can I use ChatGPT instead of Claude?

Yes — paste the same prompt; quality depends on following the rubric. Claude prompt is tested format.

## Can I use this with clients?

Yes — single **business** license allows use on client engagements. Don’t resell the template itself as a competing product.

## Why 7 dimensions?

So scores aren’t one vague “lead quality.” You see **why** a lead is HOT (pain vs access vs urgency).

## What if every lead is WATCH?

Usually **Service Catalog** doesn’t match your market, or **Evidence Signals** are thin. Improve research before outbound.

## Can I change tiers?

You can edit formulas in Airtable; default: HOT ≥90, HIGH ≥75, MEDIUM ≥60. Document changes for your team.

## Do you scrape leads for me?

No. You bring companies; the system scores and tracks outreach.
