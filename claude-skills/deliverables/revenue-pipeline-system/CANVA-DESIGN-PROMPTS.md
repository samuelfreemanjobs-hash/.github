# Canva design prompts — Revenue Pipeline System (Etsy)

**Senior designer brief:** See `ETSY-LISTING-PACKAGE-FULL.md` §3 (mockup concepts).  
**Canvas:** 2000 × 2000 px (Etsy listing images). **Banner:** 3360 × 840 px (shop header).

## Brand kit (create once in Canva)

| Token | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| Navy | `#0F172A` | Backgrounds, headlines on light |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Type on navy |
| Amber | `#D97706` | HOT tier, accents, checkmarks |
| Slate | `#64748B` | Subheads |
| Light bg | `#F8FAFC` | Screenshot slides |

**Fonts:** Inter or DM Sans · Bold headlines · Regular body · No script fonts.

**Never on creative:** income guarantees, fake ★★★ reviews, “10X revenue”, stock handshake photos.
