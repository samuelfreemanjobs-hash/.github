## Description (paste into Etsy)

**Stop buying generic “50 AI prompts” that never touch FBA inbound, PPC, or account health.**

The **FBA AI Agent Workbook** is a **structured system** for solo sellers, startups, and side hustlers who want **AI agents to help run Amazon FBA** — without handing your catalog to a black box.

You get **finished worksheets and weekly rituals**, not a loose ZIP of copy-paste prompts.

### What you get (instant download)

1. **PDF workbook** (~45–60 pages when built) — fillable-style sections you can print or use on tablet  
2. **Notion duplicate** — same structure for teams who live in Notion  
3. **3 walkthrough outlines** (Loom scripts included) — MCP/API setup at a *human-approval* level (no vendor logos in customer files)

### Inside the workbook

- **Week 0:** Agent stack map (what automates vs what you approve)  
- **Listing & catalog agent** — title/bullet drafts + *your* approval column  
- **Inbound & inventory agent** — shipment checklist + exception log  
- **PPC & ads agent** — search-term review sheet (you sign off before spend changes)  
- **Account health agent** — policy/performance weekly scan template  
- **Spreadsheet “approval gate”** — one tab per workflow (the wedge: *human-in-the-loop*)  
- **Compliance reminders** — trademarks, claims, and “don’t let the bot publish” guardrails  

### Who this is for

- Solo Amazon FBA sellers and side hustlers  
- Startup founders testing FBA as a channel  
- Sellers already using ChatGPT/Claude who need **ops structure**, not more hype  

### Who this is NOT for

- People who want fully autonomous “set and forget” bots  
- Wholesale/retail arbitrage with zero interest in workflows  
- Anyone expecting guaranteed revenue or rank outcomes  

### How to use it

1. Download PDF + Notion link from your Etsy purchase email.  
2. Complete **Week 0** (30 minutes).  
3. Run **one agent ritual per week** (60–90 min) — approve every external change in the sheet.  
4. Optional: pair with Seller Central reports you already export (no API required to start).
