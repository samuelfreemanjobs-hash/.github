## Manuscript structure

**Target length:** 35,000–50,000 words (eBook) · ~12–14 chapters + appendices  
**Tone:** Practical, skeptical of hype, step-by-step; assume reader has Seller Central access but not coding background.

### Front matter

1. **Title page**  
2. **Copyright & disclaimer** — not legal/tax advice; no Amazon affiliation; results not guaranteed  
3. **How to use this book** — Kindle + optional Gumroad bundle; one ritual per week  
4. **What you need** — Seller Central, spreadsheet, AI workspace (Claude/ChatGPT), optional API/MCP later  

### Part I — Mindset & guardrails (Ch 1–3)

| Ch | Title | Outcomes |
|----|--------|----------|
| 1 | **Why FBA Still Rewards Operators in the Agent Era** | FBA ops map: listing, inbound, PPC, account health; where agents help vs hurt |
| 2 | **The Human-in-the-Loop Rule** | Approval gate pattern; what never goes live without you |
| 3 | **Compliance & Claims** | Policies, trademarks, review manipulation traps; “agent said publish” failures |

### Part II — Your agent stack (Ch 4–6)

| Ch | Title | Outcomes |
|----|--------|----------|
| 4 | **Pick Your Layer: Chat, Scripts, API, MCP** | Decision tree; when MCP gateways make sense (disclose vendor/affiliate) |
| 5 | **The Weekly FBA Agent Calendar** | Mon–Fri micro-rituals; 90-minute weekly review |
| 6 | **Build Your Master Spreadsheet** | Tabs: Listings, Inbound, PPC, Health, Approvals; column definitions |

### Part III — Workflows (Ch 7–11)

| Ch | Title | Outcomes |
|----|--------|----------|
| 7 | **Listing & Catalog Agent** | Draft bullets/titles; image briefs; approval before submit |
| 8 | **Inbound & Inventory Agent** | Shipment plans, carton math checks, exception log |
| 9 | **PPC & Search-Term Agent** | Harvest negatives/positives; **no bid changes** without sign-off |
| 10 | **Account Health Agent** | Performance notifications checklist; voice-of-customer themes |
| 11 | **Content & Brand Agent** | A+ drafts, storefront copy; brand registry caveats |

### Part IV — Scale & channels (Ch 12–14)

| Ch | Title | Outcomes |
|----|--------|----------|
| 12 | **From Solo to Tiny Team** | Notion/handoff; what to document for VAs |
| 13 | **Route Traffic: KDP, YouTube Shorts, Forums** | Ethical promotion; no fake reviews |
| 14 | **90-Day Roadmap** | Milestones; kill criteria for automations that add risk |

### Back matter

- **Appendix A:** Sample approval sheet (copy-ready table)  
- **Appendix B:** Prompt patterns (framed as *templates inside rituals*, not standalone dump)  
- **Appendix C:** Glossary (FBA, ASIN, MCP, SP-API at high level)  
- **Appendix D:** Tool disclosure & affiliate statement  
- **About the author**  
- **Call to action:** Gumroad bundle (videos + Notion duplicate of workbook) — cross-sell with Etsy SKU if live
