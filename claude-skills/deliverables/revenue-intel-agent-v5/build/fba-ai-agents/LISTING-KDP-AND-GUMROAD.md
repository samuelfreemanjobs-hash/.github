## KDP — book description (Amazon product page)

*Run FBA with AI Agents* is for solo sellers, startup founders, and side hustlers who want **AI to help with Amazon FBA operations**—listing, inbound, PPC, and account health—**without** giving a bot the keys to your catalog.

Most “AI for Amazon” content is either generic prompts or hype about full automation. This book teaches a **human-in-the-loop system**: every external change passes through a simple **approval gate** on a spreadsheet you control.

**Inside you’ll learn how to:**

- Map where agents help vs where they create policy and brand risk  
- Run a **weekly FBA agent calendar** (60–90 minutes) built for operators, not coders  
- Use approval sheets for listings, inbound, PPC search terms, and account health  
- Choose the right layer—chat, scripts, API, or MCP—and when **not** to automate  
- Scale from solo to a tiny team with handoff-ready documentation  

**You don’t need** an API on day one. You **do need** Seller Central access, a spreadsheet, and an AI workspace (ChatGPT, Claude, or similar).

**This book is education only.** It does not promise revenue, rank, or account outcomes. It is **not** sponsored by or affiliated with Amazon. Trademark and policy reminders are included so you don’t let “the agent said publish” become an account health problem.

**Optional companion:** Video walkthroughs and duplicate templates are available separately (Gumroad bundle)—linked in the book’s back matter.
