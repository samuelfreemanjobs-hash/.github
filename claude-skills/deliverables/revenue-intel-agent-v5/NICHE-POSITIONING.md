# Positioning — Revenue Intel Agent — v5.0

## One-line promise

**A weekly revenue brief you can defend in a client meeting — dated sources, transparent math, and explicit rejections.**

## Who it's for

- Consultants and operators who sell **90-day executable** offers to a defined **niche + ICP**
- Teams running a **weekly cadence** (novelty gate + `prior_run_opportunities` prevent repetition)
- Buyers who want **precision over recall** — including zero-opportunity weeks

## What you inject each run

`today`, `niche`, `icp`, and optional `output_modes`, `search_budget`, `owner_role`, `owner_capabilities`. See `RUNTIME-CONTEXT.example.yaml`.

## Freeman Intelligence default use case

Metro Detroit industrial / automotive suppliers is a **common** niche for Sam's stack — not hard-coded in v5.0. Set niche/ICP in runtime context per run.

## vs HUNTER CRM ($97)

| Product | Job |
|---------|-----|
| **HUNTER CRM** | Score, tier, and log outreach on your pipeline |
| **Revenue Intel Agent — v5.0** | Research + model + brief new monetizable opportunities |

## Anti-personas

- Buyers who want 5 ideas every week regardless of evidence
- Fully automated outreach without human verification
- Substitute for legal, tax, or compliance counsel
