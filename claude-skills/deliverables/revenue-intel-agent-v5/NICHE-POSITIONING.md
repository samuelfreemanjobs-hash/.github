# Niche positioning — Revenue Intel Agent — v5.0

## One-line promise

**Find compliance and cost-recovery angles on Metro Detroit industrial accounts before your competitor sends the LinkedIn DM.**

## Primary ICP

| Persona | Situation | Buys this because |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Industrial integrator / automation consultant** | Sells $8K–$40K projects to tier 2–3 suppliers | Needs **specific** pain hooks, not generic “digital transformation” |
| **Fractional ops / COO** | Retainer with 2–4 plant clients | Wants weekly **intel briefs** to justify roadmap work |
| **Boutique agency (ops + data)** | Competes on spreadsheets → systems | Needs OTIF/routing/ASN vocabulary in outreach |

## Anti-personas (do not market)

- Ecommerce / DTC brands  
- Enterprise teams with dedicated Gartner-tier GTM intelligence  
- Buyers wanting guaranteed revenue or legal outcomes  
- Clinical HIPAA practices (use Clinical Revenue stack instead)

## Differentiation vs HUNTER CRM ($97)

| Product | Job |
|---------|-----|
| **HUNTER CRM** | Score, tier, and log outreach on **your** pipeline |
| **Revenue Intel Agent — v5.0** | **Generate** opportunity hypotheses and evidence for **new** targets |

Bundle story (Phase 2): Intel finds the angle → HUNTER scores and tracks the deal.

## Geography

Default **Metro Detroit industrial corridor**. User may override geo in prompt; agent must label when research leaves default region.
