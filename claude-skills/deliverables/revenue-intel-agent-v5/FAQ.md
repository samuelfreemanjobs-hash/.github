# FAQ — Revenue Intel Agent — v5.0

## Is this a chatbot app?

No. It is a **system prompt** and workflow you run in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Business OS.

## How is this different from HUNTER CRM?

HUNTER tracks and scores your pipeline. Revenue Intel **generates** opportunity hypotheses and evidence for new targets.

## Can I resell the prompt?

No. Personal / single-business seat. See `LICENSE.txt`.

## Will it browse the web?

Depends on your AI tool. The prompt assumes you enable search when available; otherwise paste public links and filings yourself.

## Is this legal or tax advice?

**No.** Intel only. Verify programs and compliance obligations with qualified professionals.

## Can I use it outside Michigan?

Yes — tell the model your geo. Default tuning is Metro Detroit industrial.

## What if outputs are wrong?

Use `confidence`, `verification_questions`, and `disqualifiers`. Never outreach without human review.
